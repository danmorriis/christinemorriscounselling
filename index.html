About Me

Getting to Know Christine

My Story

Why I became a counsellor

I've always believed that people carry more strength than they realise — they often just need a safe, unhurried space to find it again. That belief is what led me from my own experiences of life's harder chapters into training as a counsellor, so I could offer others the same steady presence I once needed myself.

My Approach

Person-centred, always

I work in a person-centred way, which means you lead and I listen — without judgement, agendas, or quick fixes. Sessions are paced around what feels right for you, drawing on integrative techniques only where they genuinely serve your process.

What happens in the first session?
We'll spend the time getting to know each other — what's brought you to counselling, what you'd like to get from it, and how I work. There's no pressure to share anything before you're ready.
How long does counselling take?
Everyone's different — some clients find a handful of sessions enough, others prefer longer-term, open-ended work. We'll review this together as we go.
Is online counselling as effective as face-to-face?
Research and client experience both suggest it can be just as effective — it often comes down to personal preference and what helps you feel most comfortable opening up.
Is everything I say confidential?
Yes, with the rare exception of a serious risk to your safety or someone else's, in line with BACP's ethical framework, which I'll explain fully in our first session.
What's your cancellation policy?
I ask for at least 48 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel a session wherever possible, out of fairness to other clients.

Person-Centred Counselling

A Space Where You Can Be Yourself

As a Person-Centred Counsellor, I believe that everyone has the ability to grow, heal and make meaningful changes in their lives when they feel truly heard, understood and accepted.

I offer a safe, supportive and non-judgemental space where you can talk openly about whatever is important to you.

Rather than giving advice or telling you what to do, I will work alongside you with empathy and understanding, helping you explore your thoughts, feelings and experiences at a pace that feels comfortable for you.

Building a trusting relationship is at the heart of counselling. My aim is to help you feel valued, accepted and supported as you gain a deeper understanding of yourself and find your own way forward.

Sometimes counselling can bring difficult emotions to the surface, and there may be times when the process feels challenging. This is a natural part of personal growth, and any concerns or questions you have are always welcome within our work together.

About Me

Meet Christine Morris

I'm a qualified Person-Centred Counsellor and Registered Member of the BACP, providing counselling for adults, young people aged 11+ and neurodivergent individuals.

Reaching out for support isn't always easy. You may be feeling overwhelmed, stuck, anxious or simply finding life difficult to navigate right now. Whatever has brought you here, I offer a safe, compassionate and non-judgemental space where you can talk freely and be yourself.

As a Person-Centred Counsellor, I believe that everyone has the capacity for growth and change when they feel genuinely heard, understood and accepted. My role is to walk alongside you as you explore your thoughts, feelings and experiences at a pace that feels right for you.

How I Work

I work with children aged 11 and above, young people and adults, offering online, telephone and face-to-face counselling. I also offer walk and talk therapy for those who feel more comfortable talking whilst outdoors and moving rather than sitting in a therapy room.

Over the years, I have supported clients experiencing a range of difficulties, including anxiety, depression and low moods, trauma, grief and loss, low self-esteem and confidence, identity and self-worth, relationship issues including family, domestic abuse, infertility and the challenges that can come with living with a chronic illness. I understand that every person's story is unique, and I will always tailor our work to your individual needs.

A particular area of interest is supporting neurodivergent children, young people and adults. I have many years of experience working alongside neurodivergent children and young people, and I understand the unique challenges that parents and carers can face. Whether you are seeking support for yourself or your child, I aim to create a space where you feel understood, valued and accepted.

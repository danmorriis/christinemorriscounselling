About Me

Meet Christine Morris

I'm a registered BACP member and Person-Centred Counsellor, working with adults and young people aged 11+. I have particular experience supporting neurodivergent clients and their families, and I bring a warm, accepting, and genuine approach to every session.

  • Registered BACP member
  • Person-Centred Counsellor
  • Work with adults and young people aged 11+
  • Experience supporting neurodivergent clients and families
My Approach

Empathy. Acceptance. Authenticity.

Empathy

Understanding your experience from your point of view, not mine.

Acceptance

A non-judgemental space, whatever you bring to it.

Authenticity

Showing up as genuinely myself, so you can too.

Your Pace

Working at the client's pace, never rushed.

Qualifications

Trained, supervised, accountable

  • Level 5 Certificate in Counselling Children and Young People
  • Level 4 Diploma in Therapeutic Counselling
  • Online and Telephone Counselling Certificate
  • Foundation Certificate in Bereavement
  • Suicide Intervention Training

Plus ongoing CPD training throughout the year.

A Personal Message

"I believe that healing begins when we feel truly heard and accepted. My aim is to offer a safe, supportive space where you can explore whatever life is bringing your way."
